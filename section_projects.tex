	\project
	{Optical-flow obstacle avoidance for a fixed-wing UAV}{2019}
	{\emph{B.Eng. (Hons) thesis -- University of Newcastle, supervised by Dr Chris Renton} \quad \github{Prelly95/Final-Year-Project}}
	{Vision-only (single-camera) obstacle avoidance that steers on the divergence of the optical-flow field.
	Derived the flow kinematics from first principles on a unit view-sphere (6-DOF camera motion, rotation
	matrices across camera/body/world frames, inverse-depth parameterisation) and posed heading selection as a
	divergence-versus-goal cost-function optimisation. Built a full camera and lens-distortion calibration
	pipeline (undistortion in C via a MATLAB MEX) and validated it in a Unity physics simulation against
	ground-truth depth.}
	{Optical Flow, Computer Vision, Kinematics, Coordinate Transforms, Camera Calibration, Optimisation, C++/OpenCV, MATLAB, C (MEX), Unity}
